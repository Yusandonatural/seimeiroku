Western Astrology

Western Astrology

Born in ancient Babylon and systematized in Greece, Western astrology reads the arrangement of the heavens at the moment of your birth as a natal chart. The Sun is said to show your purpose, the Moon your unguarded feelings, and the rising sign the first impression you give the world. This reading works your three stars out from the real sky, using astronomical approximations.

命
Four Pillars of Destiny

Four Pillars

Systematized in Song-dynasty China, the Four Pillars is the king of Eastern divination: it raises four pillars of stems and branches for the year, month, day and hour of your birth, and reads a person from the circulation of ki through the calendar. No system is finer-grained in theory, and the heart of the reading is the stem of the birth day — the Day Master, the very core of who you are.

方
Nine Star Ki & Directions

Nine Star Ki & Directions

Nine Star Ki assigns to your birth year one of nine stars derived from the Luoshu, the legendary diagram of ancient China, and reads direction and timing from it. It was perfected in Japan as kigaku in the Taisho era. Because the nine stars travel the board each year, both your season of fortune and your lucky directions shift from year to year.

Animal Fortune — 60 Characters

Animal Fortune

Animal Fortune is a personality system born in 1990s Japan, built on the twelve stages of the Four Pillars. Its sixty characters map one-to-one onto the sixty stem-branch pairs, so behind the charming animals stands the skeleton of a serious Eastern art. It is an excellent doorway for grasping someone's social style at a glance.

数
Numerology

Numerology — Your Birth Number and Life Path

Numerology is the mysticism of number attributed to the ancient Greek mathematician Pythagoras. All is number — and so the blueprint of a life is said to be engraved in the single digit to which a birth date or a name reduces. Only 11, 22 and 33 are left unreduced, as the special master numbers.

智
Kabbalah

Kabbalistic Numerology — the Tree of Life

Kabbalah is the mystical tradition of Judaism, which pictures the structure of the universe as the Tree of Life: a diagram of ten spheres called sefirot. It shares a root with numerology, but reads each number as a spiritual map — asking where on the tree your soul is standing.

暦
Mayan Tzolkin

Mayan Calendar — Your KIN and Solar Seal

The Tzolkin is the 260-day sacred calendar the priests of the ancient Maya used in ritual. Twenty solar seals and thirteen tones weave 260 KIN that turn through the days, and the KIN of your birth is said to be the blueprint of your soul. This reading uses the Dreamspell method that spread in modern times, which does not count leap days.

宿
Shukuyo 27 Mansions

Shukuyo Astrology — The Twenty-Seven Mansions

Shukuyo astrology is esoteric Buddhist astrology: Indian star lore that travelled through China as the Xiuyao Sutra and was brought to Japan in the Heian era by Kukai. It reads essence and compatibility from the twenty-seven mansions in which the Moon lodges. So well did it strike its mark, the story goes, that warlords used it for strategy and the Edo shogunate forbade its use among the people.

算
Sanmeigaku

Sanmeigaku — The Body Star Chart

Sanmeigaku is a science of destiny rooted in the Chinese theory of yin-yang and the five elements, and systematized in Japan. From the stems and branches of your birth date it draws a body star chart, reading character and the flow of a life from the stars that dwell in five places: head, chest, belly and both hands. It has long been studied as a leadership art in politics and business.

六
Six Star Astrology

Rokusei Fortune-telling — Your Destiny Star and Its Seasons

Rokusei fortune-telling was brought to its final form by Kazuko Hosoki on the foundations of Sanmeigaku and related arts. It sorts a birth date into six destiny stars — Saturn, Venus, Mars, Uranus, Jupiter and Mercury people — and is widely known for reading a twelve-year cycle of fortune, above all the three-year winter known as the Great Ill Period.

型
Enneagram

Enneagram — The Nine Personality Types

The Enneagram is a nine-type theory of personality whose roots lie in ancient wisdom and which was woven into modern psychology in the twentieth century. Its name comes from the figure formed by joining nine points around a circle. Used in corporate training worldwide, it is a map for self-understanding; properly, you find your own type through honest self-observation.
